## Local storage in your browser

When you switch between tabs on a page, your browser stores the selection in its `localStorage`,
so that it is kept for your next visit.
This data does not leave your browser and can be deleted at any time in the browser settings.
The storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).
